# Privacy Policy for Highlighter

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Highlighter is a Chrome extension for highlighting text on webpages and PDFs. This policy explains what data the
extension handles and where that data goes.

**In short: your highlights stay on your device. If you turn on sync, they are also stored in your own Google Drive.
The developer runs no server and never receives your data.**

## What the extension stores

| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Text you highlight, with your notes, tags, collections and colors | To show and organise your highlights | On your device |
| The address (URL) and title of each page or PDF you highlight | To show highlights again when you return, and to link back to the source | On your device |
| PDF files you import into the viewer | To display the PDF and its highlights | On your device |
| Your Google account name, email address and profile picture, if you sign in | To show which account is signed in | On your device |
| Your settings (default color, theme, sync options) | To remember your preferences | On your device |

The extension reads the text of pages you visit only to find where your own highlights belong. It does not record
your browsing history, clicks, keystrokes or any page you have not highlighted.

## Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off by default. Signing in and turning on sync are separate steps, and sync asks for its own permission.

When sync is on, the highlight data listed above is uploaded to a hidden application folder in **your own** Google
Drive, so your other devices can download it. PDF files are uploaded only if you also turn on "Also back up imported
PDF files".

The extension uses the Google Drive permission `drive.appdata`. This lets it read and write only its own hidden
folder. It cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive.

The extension's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send your data to the developer or to any server other than Google's, and only to Google when you sign
  in or turn on sync.
- It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
- It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

## Deleting your data

- Delete individual highlights in the dashboard.
- **Sign out & disconnect** in Dashboard → Settings revokes the extension's access to your Google account and stops
  sync.
- Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything stored on that device.
- To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find Highlighter and choose
  **Delete hidden app data**.
